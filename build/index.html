      How to play

      1. Find shows the scale degree to locate; Current is where you are.
      2. Tap that degree anywhere on the neck — several spots count.
      3. A correct tap plays the real note and moves you on. Build a streak.

      Change the key, hide the note labels to make it harder, or try Time Attack.

      Know the neck, not just the shapes

      Most guitarists memorise a few shapes and hope. Fretboardwalk trains the thing underneath them — knowing where you are anywhere on the neck, in any key.

      You're shown a number, you find it, you hear the real note. Free, no sign-up.

      You can start the tour later from the ? button.

      Nice run — ready to make it harder?

      Hide the numbers and you have to recall the shape instead of reading it. That's where the neck actually sticks.

      Turn your phone upright

      Fretboardwalk runs the neck vertically, so it needs portrait. Rotate back to carry on.
